1.7 — Profile Photo (Optional)

  • Profile photo — if you choose to personalize your profile, you can take a new photo with your camera, choose an existing photo from your device's gallery, or pick one of our built-in illustrated avatars instead
  • If you take or choose a photo, it is automatically resized/compressed on your device and any location (GPS) or other device metadata (EXIF data) embedded in the photo is stripped out before it is uploaded
  • Uploaded photos are stored on our own servers — never shared with, or stored by, any third party or advertiser
  • Built-in illustrated avatars are drawn entirely within the app itself; choosing one does not upload or transmit any photo or image at all

📌 Camera & Photo Gallery Permission: If you choose to take or upload a photo, your device will ask you to grant camera or photo gallery access. This permission is used solely so you can capture or select your profile photo — we do not access your camera or gallery at any other time or for any other purpose, and adding a profile photo is entirely optional.

1.8 — Country & City (Regional Usage Data)

  • Country — determined automatically from the international dial code of the WhatsApp number you provide at sign-up (e.g. a number starting with +92 is recorded as Pakistan). This is not derived from GPS, IP address, or any other device location data
  • City — a fully optional field you may choose to fill in yourself from Profile → Edit Profile; it stays blank unless you type something in
  • We use this information only in aggregate — for example, seeing which countries and cities our overall user base is concentrated in — to make informed decisions about where to focus our own marketing and advertising campaigns
  • This data is never sold or shared with advertisers, ad networks, or any other third party, and is never used to individually track or target you with ads (see "What We Do NOT Do" in the next section)
  • Visible only to our internal team for business analytics — never shown publicly to other users of the app, including in the community chat
  • You can update or clear your city at any time from Profile → Edit Profile, or request full deletion of this data via the Account Deletion section above
📌 Why We Collect This: Knowing which countries and cities our users are concentrated in helps us decide where to run advertising to grow the app — for example, prioritizing a city where we already see strong organic usage. This is a business decision based on aggregated, non-identifying statistics — it is not a way of profiling, tracking, or individually targeting any single user with ads.
📌 Signal Data Flow: The Expert Advisor (EA) software, when running on your device, connects outbound to our API to receive signal data and, only if you choose to connect an account (see Section 1.9 below), to send back a live account snapshot for display inside the app. The EA never transmits your broker password/investor password.

1.9 — Trading Account Data (Optional — Live Balance Feature via EA)

If you choose to connect your MT5 account to the EA and use the optional "Live Balance" feature in the EA section of the app, your EA sends the following read-only account snapshot to our servers so we can display it back to you inside the app, purely for your own convenience and monitoring:

  • MT5 account number and account name
  • Live balance, equity, and floating profit/loss
  • Commission, swap, and total deposit/withdrawal totals
  • Realized profit history (15 days, 1 month, 2 months, all-time)
  • Connection status and last sync (heartbeat) time
📌 This Feature Is Entirely Opt-In: This data is only sent if you install the EA on your own MT5 terminal and it is actively connected/attached to an account — nothing is sent otherwise. We use this snapshot solely to show you your own account's live balance, equity, and profit/loss inside the app, so you can monitor how the EA is performing on your account without opening MetaTrader separately. We do not use it for any other purpose, we do not share it with any third party, and we never receive your MT5 password or investor password — the EA only reads the account statistics that MT5 itself exposes locally, and only your account number/name plus these balance figures are transmitted.

🧹 Spam Prevention & Account Moderation

We want Trading Insights to stay a clean and trustworthy place for genuine users. To do that, we use a limited set of account and device information to detect and act on spam, fake accounts, duplicate accounts, and abusive behaviour.

What we look at

  • WhatsApp number, device identifier, device model/OS and IP address — to spot fake, duplicate, or repeat sign-ups
  • Sign-in and account activity (for example, when and from which device an account was used)
  • Reports submitted by other users, and the details of any moderation action taken

Who can see it, and what can happen

  • Administrative and moderation tools are available only to authorized Trading Insights staff, and only to keep the service safe and to support users
  • If an account is found to be spam, fake, or in breach of our Terms, we may warn, restrict, suspend, or permanently remove it

What we keep after an account is removed

If an account is removed for spam, fraud, or serious abuse — or if a person deletes their account after being flagged — we may keep a minimal anti-abuse record (such as the device identifier, WhatsApp number or account identifier, the reason, and the date) for up to 24 months, so the same person cannot immediately return and repeat the behaviour. This record is kept for security and fraud-prevention purposes only. Apart from records we are legally required to keep (such as payment records), it is the only personal data we retain after account deletion.

📌 Purpose Limitation: This information is used only for security, spam prevention, and keeping the community safe. It is never used for marketing or advertising, never sold, and never used to profile or analyse your trading.

4️⃣ Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable and industry-standard steps to protect your personal data:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS

  • Server access is restricted to authorized personnel only

⚠️ No system is completely immune to security risks. While we implement strong safeguards, we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account password and for any activity under your account.
